Terminus 0.2.85 goes back to the stable 0.2.82 while we fix the problems in 0.2.84.

- **The 0.2.82 experience is back.** The new onboarding, signing in with your Danil account on the desktop, the new sidebar and the other changes from 0.2.83 and 0.2.84 are removed for now. Your tasks, folders and accounts stay as they were.
- **Updating no longer fails with "service_busy".** If the previous version is still working, Terminus waits for it to finish what is in progress, without cutting anything, and opens on its own when it is done.
